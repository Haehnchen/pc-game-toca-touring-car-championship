Mega HUD vector artwork

primary-km.webp, primary-miles.webp, secondary.webp, caption-big.webp,
caption-small.webp, menufont-0..3.webp and car-name.webp are newly drawn artwork.
The authoring recipe is tools/build-hud-assets.py. Its specified numeral
contours, letter outlines, bevel ramps, backgrounds and smooth tachometer curves
follow the appearance and layout of TOCA1.1a's original HUD. No installed font,
enlarged original bitmap or original bitmap pixels are used in the output.

The reference is the unchanged data/image640.bfp,
CRC32 1060ace3, and the original executable's sprite rectangle tables.
Car names use ALLCARS.BFP (CRC32 92273ec9), member0 fonts\win3.fon
(CRC32 afa81da8), as their sole source for cells, metrics and visual design.
Read-only visual exports can be generated beneath build/hud-art-original/.
They are authoring references and must not be committed as game assets.

Regenerate the ten vector atlases with Pillow:
  python3 tools/build-hud-assets.py

Generate the same runtime files, a complete labelled overview and reusable
individual sprite cutouts with their source-rectangle/CRC32 manifest:
  python3 tools/build-hud-assets.py --sprites build/hud-art/sprites --preview build/hud-art/overview.png

Use --output DIR for a separate atlas destination. Normal generation reads only
the saved geometry scripts; it does not need extracted reference images.
Large numeral Bezier paths and the small font live in tools/hud_font_geometry.py.
The46 BIG640 caption glyphs live in tools/hud_caption_geometry.py; the original
member23 reference checksum is399cab32.
The61 upright LIL640 glyphs live in tools/hud_small_caption_geometry.py;
the original member24 checksum is29d0f179. Frontend OPTION3/OPTION3S/HELP3/TITLE3
live in tools/hud_menu_font_geometry.py, including the native added parentheses.
The38 WIN3 car-name glyphs live in tools/car_name_font_geometry.py. Its native
upright contours retain the heavy stems, narrow counters and shallow rounded
bowls of WIN3, with the original untailed Q and no invented characters.
Labels, colors and the RPM curves live in tools/build-hud-assets.py; named
cutouts and the contact sheet are handled by tools/hud_art_exports.py.

HUD, caption and frontend atlases are four times their original coordinates.
Car-name artwork alone is exactly twice its original coordinates: a512x512
atlas, with unchanged normalized glyph cells and original ink bounds. The
runtime name strip is256x32. White RGB and antialiased alpha supply clean
edges without an outline, shadow, tint or enlargement of original pixels.
HUD and game fonts are1024x1024. The four smaller frontend atlases retain their
original dimensions, including OPTION3/OPTION3S's appended parentheses row.
Supersampling antialiases new vector geometry at that target resolution.
Colors preserve the cyan/red/yellow numerals, pale bevels, white labels,
near-black original strips and original tachometer sequence. Gear and the MpH/
KpH units use separately measured original contours and baselines, including
the lowercase p's complete descender. Small12-pixel digits use their own
upright masters with shared rounded bowls, stroke widths and one common italic
shear, with upright punctuation and the original cap height/advances. English MpH and
the other languages' KpH differ only in the original speed-unit region.
Small red/yellow digits and punctuation retain the original one-pixel gray8
outline inside each source cell, shared by times, speed, lap limits and splits.
All rectangles used by the current HUD are included; unused brand logos and
non-HUD sprites are excluded. Original source rectangles, destinations,
value formatting and simulation logic are handled by the unchanged HUD rules.
The RPM band uses an analytic ellipse and a constant12-unit inward normal
offset. Twelve sectors are cut from that regular band into the original atlas
cells; idle and illuminated states use the same silhouette and uniformly thin
dividers. Generation rejects a sector that would be cropped by its source cell.
The lap-limit separator is visibly pale with a thin dark outline in its original
source rectangle, as requested; the original lap backing remains complete.

caption-big.webp and caption-small.webp use a neutral white face and gray8
outline. The common font loader restores each caller's original face/edge palette:
red race/pause titles, white ESC-menu/result rows and white/black honors/credits.
The first three frontend fonts are neutral white alpha masks for the original
menu palette; TITLE3 retains its dark metallic bevel. Every original cell and
text metric remains unchanged, including accented letters, Spanish inverted
punctuation and the underlined ordinal. Glyphs are antialiased independently
before packing, with a transparent texel border against neighboring shadows.
Only graphics profile Mega selects these atlases; other profiles retain all
original fonts.

New output uses WebP quality70, method6 and alpha quality100. vector-art.json records the
recipe and output CRC32 checksums. The other three start-light assets and their
provenance are authored separately; this tool does not read or modify them.
